Attribution: individual Mozilla / MDN contributors.
Original source URLs for every bundled snippet are retained in data.js.

MDN's published code-sample policy distinguishes samples introduced on/after
20 August 2010 (CC0) from older samples (MIT). Its policy also notes that the
current site does not expose enough history to determine this per sample.
This distribution therefore retains source attribution and the MIT terms
below, rather than asserting that every historical sample is definitely CC0.
No MDN article prose, logos or website design are included in the code corpus.
